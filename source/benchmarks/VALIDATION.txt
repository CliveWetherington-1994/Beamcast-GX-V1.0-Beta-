Performance/correctness fixes in 4.1
------------------------------------
- GX report byte accounting no longer exports/copies the complete scene twice per render.
- HX fixed local traversal stack now spills safely instead of silently dropping BVH nodes.
- Framebuffer hot-path at() remains unchecked for renderer performance; checked_at() is available for user-facing bounds checks.
- HX progress callbacks are serialized.
- Uniform-grid dimension multiplication is checked before allocation.

Archive verification
--------------------
PASS: final source archive extracted into a clean directory, configured, rebuilt, and passed 8/8 tests.
