Primary reasons
---------------
- removed shared per-ray atomic telemetry increment
- per-worker local ray counts with coarse merging
- dynamic one-tile scheduling for path-cost load balance
- fewer atomic cancellation/generation checks
- parallel final tone map / framebuffer conversion
- no temporal-history copies unless temporal reconstruction is enabled
- post effects parallelized across available cores
- storage reuse for history/guide buffers

Scope
-----
These are local measurements for the listed workloads and environment. They do not imply identical scaling on every CPU, OS, scene, or memory configuration.
