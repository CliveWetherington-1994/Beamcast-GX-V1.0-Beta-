- 40-byte node -> 32-byte node
- 36-byte material -> 32-byte material
- 68-byte 5.7 sphere candidate stream -> 36-byte 5.8 stream (~47.1% less)
- 68-byte 5.7 triangle candidate stream -> 44-byte 5.8 stream (~35.3% less)
- guide payload 16 -> 12 bytes/pixel (25% less)
- color+guide readback 32 -> 28 bytes/pixel (12.5% less)

-------------------

Actual GPU timing should be measured with Beamcast's integrated GPU/GIGA benchmark on the destination machine.
