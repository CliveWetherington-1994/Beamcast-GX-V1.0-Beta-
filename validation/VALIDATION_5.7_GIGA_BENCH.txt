Score definition
----------------
GIGA Score = 100 * geometric mean of measured Mray/s across ray-producing benchmark cases.
An OpenCL GPU probe is included in the score only when backend telemetry confirms OpenCL execution.
The score is only intended for comparison using the same Beamcast suite/build.
