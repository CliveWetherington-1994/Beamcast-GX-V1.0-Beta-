Hardware-validation limitation
------------------------------
This execution environment does not expose a usable physical OpenCL GPU. The Windows OpenCL dispatcher and device kernel are compiled/syntax-validated, but end-to-end GPU execution and performance must be measured on the destination machine with a usable OpenCL GPU driver/runtime.

No claim of 'best optimization in the world' is made. That would require reproducible benchmarks against established renderers across multiple hardware classes and workloads.
