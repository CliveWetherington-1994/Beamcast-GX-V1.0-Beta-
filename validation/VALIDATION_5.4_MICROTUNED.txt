Beamcast GX 5.4 MicroTuned Research validation
===============================================
Date: 2026-09-15

Primary optimization benchmark
------------------------------
Fixed workload:
- 320x180
- 6 spp
- 6 bounces
- CPU path tracing
- MIS enabled
- power-weighted light selection enabled
- Random sampler
- denoise/post disabled
- progressive framebuffer updates disabled
- adaptive sampling disabled
- 1x stochastic MSAA

5.3 median: 119.308250 ms/render
5.4 median: 88.069112 ms/render
Measured median render-time reduction: 26.18%
Measured throughput improvement: 1.355x

Important: this is a local benchmark result, not a guarantee for every scene or CPU.

5.4 micro-optimizations retained
--------------------------------
- dedicated any-hit shadow/occlusion BVH traversal
- smaller Hit record; winning hit position reconstructed from ray + t
- RenderSettings passed by pointer through hot integrator paths
- trig-free cosine hemisphere sampler via disk rejection + Malley projection
- Marsaglia uniform sphere-direction sampler
- precomputed ray direction length squared and reciprocal reused by sphere tests
- 16-entry local BVH traversal stacks with recursive overflow fallback
- Schlick x^5 evaluated by multiplies instead of generic pow
- custom RNG experiment rejected and removed after no measurable benefit
